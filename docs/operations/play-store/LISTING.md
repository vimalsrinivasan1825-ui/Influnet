## Full description (4000 max)
Influnet is where Indian creators and brands do deals — from the first request to the final payment — without losing it in DMs, spreadsheets and screenshots.

FOR CREATORS
• Get collaboration requests from brands, see the brief, and accept or decline in a tap.
• Talk terms in a built-in chat, then turn the deal into a project both sides agree to.
• Your public profile link: followers, engagement, past work and brand ratings, ready to share in your bio.
• Get the Verified badge by proving you own your Instagram account.
• See who viewed your profile — brands and creators.
• Browse open campaigns and apply.

FOR BRANDS
• Find creators by their profile link and send a request with your brief.
• Run every collaboration as a clear project: content plan, drafts, review, revisions, posting and payment.
• Both sides sign off each stage, so nothing moves without agreement.
• Pay at milestones through Razorpay — advance and final payments open only when the payment is confirmed.

BUILT FOR TRUST
• Report and block on profiles, campaigns, requests, projects and chat.
• Payments are processed by Razorpay, an RBI-licensed payment aggregator; card and UPI details never touch our servers.
• Delete your account any time from Settings.

Influnet is for users aged 18 and over.
