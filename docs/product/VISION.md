# Influnet — Product Vision

> Influnet is a premium influencer-marketing platform that removes the latency between businesses and creators. Creators put their Influnet link-in-bio; businesses click it and start a collaboration in minutes, not days.

## Why Influnet?

### The Problem

Today, creators and businesses mostly connect through DMs and emails.

* Average response time: Several hours to 1 day.
* Many messages receive no response at all.
* Large creators often have managers or admins handling inboxes, causing further delays.
* Businesses lose opportunities while waiting, and creators miss potential collaborations.

The Influnet Solution

Influnet instantly notifies creators whenever a business sends a collaboration request.

This allows conversations to begin within minutes instead of days.

Benefits for Creators

* Receive verified collaboration requests instantly.
* No need to constantly monitor Instagram DMs or emails.
* Connect with the right brands faster.
* Spend less time searching and more time creating.

Benefits for Business Owners

* Get faster responses from creators.
* Reduce follow-ups and communication headaches.
* Start campaigns quickly.
* Prevent opportunities from going to competing brands due to delayed responses.

How It Works

1. Register on Influnet.
2. Add your Influnet profile link to your social media bio/description.
3. Receive and manage collaboration requests in one place.
4. Build successful partnerships and grow your network.

Influnet Mission

Less waiting. Less effort. More collaborations. More success.
